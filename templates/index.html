About Meaw Loader

Meaw Loader is a free, privacy-first web utility designed for fast, high-quality video and audio archival. Powered by yt-dlp, it extracts raw video streams and high-bitrate audio from over 1,000 supported platforms directly to your device without invasive popups or third-party ads.

🎬 HD Video & 4K Support

Download crisp MP4 video streams with granular resolution selection from 360p up to 1080p and 4K, complete with live estimated file sizes.

🎵 MP3 Audio Extraction

Instantly convert clips, music, podcasts, and speeches into pristine 192–320kbps MP3 audio files with clean metadata.

⚡ Real-Time Progress Bar

Monitor downloads dynamically with live server-side progress percentage tracking, speed calculation, and responsive indicators.

🔒 Offline & Private History

Your download logs stay strictly in your browser’s localStorage. Sort your previous files by date, title, or file size and re-download anytime.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download videos with Meaw Loader?
Simply copy the URL of any video or playlist from supported platforms, paste it into the box above, choose MP4 (video) or MP3 (audio), and click Grab. Then pick your desired resolution and hit Download.
Is Meaw Loader completely free?
Yes, Meaw Loader is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, registration requirements, or download quotas.
Which sites are supported?
Over 1,000 websites are supported through yt-dlp, including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Reddit, Facebook, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, Pinterest, Loom, and Dailymotion.
Can I download multiple links at once?
Yes! Paste multiple URLs separated by spaces, commas, or new lines. Meaw Loader will automatically deduplicate and queue them all.
Can I import a text file (.txt) with URLs?
Yes! Click the "Import TXT" button or drag and drop any text file (.txt) containing URLs directly into the URL box. Meaw Loader extracts all valid links and automatically adds them to the download queue.
